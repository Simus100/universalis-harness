A single continuous macro shot in dark water after turbulence has stopped. The remaining ink sinks:
the residual cloud falls slowly downward under gravity and thins out, leaving clear black water above
it. Out of that settling ink, exactly three horizontal filaments of light condense and solidify, one
after the other, evenly spaced, perfectly straight, exactly parallel to the horizon, and they lock
into place in the lower third of the frame. Each filament is a thin glowing thread, cold blue-white
with a faint violet edge, razor sharp, with halation. Above them the upper half of the frame stays
empty near-black water, and the centre of the frame stays a clean dark negative space. Camera: 100mm
macro lens, f/2.8, at eye level, a very slow continuous push-in, no shake, no handheld, no rotation.
Light: dim cold ambient only, the filaments are their own source. Look: shot on 35mm film, fine
natural grain, deep teal-blue shadows, no warm colours, no orange, low saturation, high dynamic
range. The material is liquid ink in water, NOT smoke, NOT dust, NOT neon tubes, NOT laser beams, NOT
hologram. Exactly three lines: not two, not four, not five. No text, no letters, no words, no people,
no objects, no logos, no watermark, no cuts, one single continuous take, 8 seconds.
