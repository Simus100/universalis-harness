A single continuous macro shot in dark water: a razor-thin blade of pure white light sweeps across
the frame on a perfect 45-degree diagonal, from the lower left corner to the upper right corner, once
only, in the first three seconds, then holds its final position. The blade is flat, perfectly
straight, with zero thickness and a thin dark gap along its edge, and it does not bend, does not
wobble, does not rotate, does not pulse: it moves in a straight line at constant speed. It cuts a
cloud of deep indigo ink cleanly in two: the ink above the diagonal turns icy blue-white, the ink
below turns violet, and no plume of ink crosses the line. Bright halation bleeds around the blade.
Camera: locked off, absolutely static, 100mm macro, f/2.8, no movement at all, no zoom, no shake.

film, fine natural grain, deep teal-blue shadows, no warm colours, no orange, low saturation, high
dynamic range. The material is liquid ink in water, NOT smoke, NOT particles, NOT energy beams, NOT
laser, NOT explosion, NOT lens flare star. No text, no letters, no words, no people, no objects, no
